Questions

Straight up.

Does it run in the cloud?

No. Stakeout runs on your own computer and saves clips there. It needs an internet connection to watch streams and to check your subscription.

Which platforms does it clip?

Twitch, for now.

Does it post the clips for me?

No. You review the clips and post the ones you like, wherever you like.

Can I clip any streamer?

Stakeout can record any public Twitch stream, but you're responsible for having the right to post what you clip. Check each streamer's rules on clipping and reposting.

What do I need?

Windows 10 or 11, or a Mac with an M1 chip or newer. The more streamers you watch at once, the more your computer works, and older or low-powered laptops take longer to make each clip.

How does the free trial work?

You enter a card at checkout and pay nothing for 7 days. On day 8 it becomes A$39 a month, unless you cancel before then.
